Unity Projects

My first interaction with coding was through Unity, which makes these projects especially meaningful to me. I usually build them as a hobby whenever I dream up a game or a new idea pops into my head, I try to bring it to life here and sometimes in GameJams XD.

This is my first Game Jam project, created during a 2-day DOGE Game Jam. Everything you see in this clip is my contribution to the game. The concept is simple: you must transport an ill patient to the hospital within a limited time. (The timer isn't visible in this clip, but it works in the actual game!)
